Why people call it the best

Roasted twice a week

Coffee is at its best within a few weeks of roasting. Ours rarely sits on a shelf for more than a few days.

Fairly sourced

We buy directly from farming cooperatives in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, and we pay above fair-trade minimums.

Made by people who care

Our baristas train for six weeks before serving a single customer. Every drink is weighed, timed, and tasted.

Plenty of options

Oat, almond, soy, and whole milk are all available. Decaf is roasted in-house, too.
