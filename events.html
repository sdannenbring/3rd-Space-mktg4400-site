Community Events in Iowa City

Every week, 3rd Space opens its tables, its stage, and its couches to the neighborhood. All events are free to attend, and everyone is welcome.

Find your kind of night

Pick a category to filter the schedule.

🎤Music & Arts

Open Mic Night

Every Thursday, 7:00 PM

Sing, read poetry, tell a story, or just cheer. Sign up at the counter when you arrive. Hosted by Priya.

📖Family & Kids

Story Time and Hot Cocoa

Every Saturday, 10:00 AM

Picture books, silly voices, and kid-size cocoa for ages 3 to 7. Grown-ups get a free drip coffee.

🗣️Learning

Language Swap Table

Every Tuesday, 6:30 PM

Practice Spanish, Arabic, Mandarin, French, or English with friendly neighbors. Beginners are very welcome.

🧠Social

Trivia Night

Every Wednesday, 7:30 PM

Teams of up to five compete for bragging rights and a free pastry for the winning table.

☕Learning

Home Barista Workshop

First Sunday of the month, 2:00 PM

Theo teaches pour-over, milk steaming, and latte art basics. Small groups, and everyone tastes as they learn.

📚Social

Silent Book Club

Every Monday, 6:00 PM

Bring a book, grab a drink, and read together in good company. Chat afterward if you like.

🎨Music & Arts

Sketch and Sip

Second Friday of the month, 6:00 PM

Supplies provided. No experience needed. Draw from a new still life each month and keep your art.

✏️Family & Kids

Teen Study Hall

Sunday through Thursday, 4:00 PM

A quiet corner with free Wi-Fi, snacks, and volunteer tutors for high school students.

Want to host something?

Do you lead a club, teach a skill, or have a great idea for a gathering? Priya is always looking for new voices. Stop by the counter and ask for the community host, and we'll find you a time and a table.
